About This Guide

This guide walks through the entire Bible in the New Living Translation across two companion tracks: the Old Testament (183 days, about six months at 5–6 chapters a day) and the New Testament (91 days, about three months at roughly three chapters a day).

Every day is written as a complete, stand-alone sermonette, so you can go deep without needing to leave the page: the historical and cultural setting, a chapter-by-chapter exposition, the central theological truth named plainly, a paraphrased insight from a trusted expositor, an honest application question, and a closing prayer.

Expositor insights are paraphrased summaries of each teacher's well-documented public teaching on the passage, not verbatim quotations — this respects copyright and gives you an accurate on-ramp to their fuller original work if it resonates. Scripture itself is referenced by chapter and verse rather than reproduced at length, so keep your own Bible open alongside this guide for the text itself.
